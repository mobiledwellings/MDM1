Meet Chuck Cassady (you probably already know him but say hi anyway). A long time ago he bought a transit bus with little idea how to drive it.

The next day, sixteen strangers piled in and drove to Tennessee.

Photos courtesy of Chuck CassadySwipe →

Chuck was a philosophy student and paying rent was not really his thing. After starting a party-bus company he had an idea.

A couple weeks later he moved into his bus.

He ended up building a home so cozy his mom encouraged him to apply to be on a Tiny Home TV Show (moms rock).

He sent in what he calls a cheeky application.

They picked him, partly because nobody else on the show was converting a school bus. Some might call Chuck a pioneer but he’s always known he stands on the shoulders of the legends who came before him.

Building one bus for television turned into building them for everybody else too, and his company Chrome Yellow Corp went on to build about 40 conversions.

Knowing he wouldn’t build forever he started a Youtube channel to pass on his knowledge and filmed over 200 videos and livestreams.

I’ve heard a dozen people say they built their bus “the Chuck Cassady way”.

Ten years of finishing other people’s buses was fulfilling, he says, but it kept pulling him away from the part he actually likes.

Which is travelling.

He bought a dog-nose short bus with the legendary DT466 (a big boy engine) and started to build a home he could travel in.

Eventually, Chuck completed his masterpiece. His advice? “Don’t worry about the money so much. You’re never going to have enough of it.”

Four years of weekends and about $60,000 in materials later.

Now Chuck travels in his rig and helps people finish theirs on site. For the full tour check out our Youtube channel and subscribe for more!
