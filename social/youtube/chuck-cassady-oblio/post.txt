Post text:

Chuck Cassady spent about a decade building other people's buses. Oblio is the
first one he had the time to build for himself.

It started in 2007 with a Craigslist ad and a ride to a festival. He was a
philosophy student who wasn't into paying rent, so he moved into the bus. Seven
years and three buses later he decided to do one properly, a tiny house show
picked him, and people started asking him to build theirs. That turned into a
conversion company, two hundred videos teaching other people how, and enough
builds that they call it the Chuck Cassady way.

The catch is that finishing buses for other people kept him away from the part
he likes most, which is travelling.

He bought Oblio in 2021. A 1996 International, 27 and a half feet, twelve inch
roof raise, four years of weekends and about $60,000 in materials.

Full tour is on the channel. The written story is here:
https://mobiledwellings.media/stories/chuck-cassady-oblio
